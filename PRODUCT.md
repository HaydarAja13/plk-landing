# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Contractors & Project Estimators: Looking for SNI certified materials (steel, wire) at wholesale prices, requiring quick quote calculations (RAB).
- Individual Customers/DIYers: Looking for reliable tools (grinders, screwdrivers, drills) for home repair with low purchase friction.

## Product Purpose
Provide a highly professional and trust-building landing page that catalogizes premium tools and construction materials, allowing customers to easily explore products and submit quotes or make purchases via WhatsApp.

## Positioning
The only premium industrial hardware supplier combining a comprehensive, ready-stock product catalog with a streamlined wholesale-to-WhatsApp quote pipeline (RAB submissions).

## Operating Context
Web browsed on both mobile (on-site contractors) and desktop (office estimators). Seamless transition from web cataloging to direct WhatsApp communication with sales representatives.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static product catalog defined in TSX with search and category filtering.
- Direct CTA linking to pre-populated WhatsApp query templates.
- Strict adherence to the corporate color palette and visual assets.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Putra Logam Kencana (PLK).
- Colors: Obsidian Black (`#0E1117`), Dark Steel (`#161B22`), Kencana Gold (`#D4AF37`), Metallic Silver (`#C0C0C0`), Accent Red (`#EF4444`).
- Logo: `/logo-plk.png` (PNG format with transparent/dark background).

## Evidence on Hand
- Curated list of high-quality tools and construction material products inside `src/data/products.ts`.
- Sidoarjo-based physical store Google Maps location embeddable iframe.

## Product Principles
1. **Industrial Precision**: Align alignment, typography, and spacing to reflect clean engineering and heavy-duty steel workmanship.
2. **Minimal Friction**: Search, filter, and pagination must be lightning-fast, and CTAs must immediately lead to actionable chats.
3. **Structured Clarity**: High scanning readability via balanced contrast, clear headers, and clean cards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High color contrast against the dark background.
- Clean font scaling and readable font sizes for contractors viewing on mobile screens on-site.
